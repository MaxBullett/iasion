# Event storming: R1 (manual ledger)

Status: draft, not yet reviewed by the domain expert.

Written event storming against the R1 stories (S-001 to S-014,
[story-map.md](../story-map.md#stories)). It is the input for the context map in
`architecture.md`. Nothing here decides an open point: conflicts, unknowns and "it
depends" are collected under [Hotspots](#hotspots), and each points to an ADR or an
open question.

## How to read this

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Actor | Who issues a command. |
| Command | A request to change something. It may be rejected. |
| Event | Something that happened, in past tense. |
| Policy | "Whenever X, then Y": a reaction the system performs without being asked. |
| Read model | What an actor must see before deciding, or what a view shows. |
| Aggregate | The consistency boundary that accepts or rejects a command, with the invariants it enforces. |
| Hotspot | A conflict, unknown or "it depends". `H-nn`, listed at the end. |

- Events are a modelling device here. Whether any of them is persisted as an event is
  not decided (H-23).
- Every rule cites the story, ADR or spec it comes from. A rule without a source is a
  hotspot, not a rule.
- Examples use the reference household (`fixtures/README.md`).
- Terms marked † are not in the glossary yet; see
  [Proposed glossary entries](#proposed-glossary-entries).

## Big picture

Events in the order a household meets them:

1. Household created, Opening party created, Member added
2. Party registered
3. Account registered
4. Opening balance recorded
5. Transaction recorded
6. Purpose defined, Purpose renamed, Purpose assigned
7. Checkpoint recorded
8. Transaction corrected, Transaction voided, Opening balance corrected, Checkpoint
   corrected, Checkpoint voided

What must exist first:

- Account registered needs its owners (Member added) and, if it has one, its
  institution (Party registered).
- Opening balance recorded needs the account and the opening party.
- Transaction recorded needs the accounts and parties it names, and must not be dated
  before the opening balance of an account it touches (S-005). Whether it needs an
  opening balance at all is H-10.
- Purpose assigned needs the purpose and the flow.
- Checkpoint recorded needs the account and must not be dated before its opening
  balance (S-011).

The only actor in R1 is a member, plus Cloudflare Access as an external system
(S-014). Nothing in R1 is triggered by time.

## 1. Household (S-001)

**Actor:** member. Who acts before any member exists is H-01.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Create household, with its first members | Household created; Member added (once per member); Opening party created | H-01 |
| Add member | Member added | not stated |

**Policies**

- Whenever a household is created, then its opening party is created (S-004,
  ADR-0011).

**Read models**

- Whether a household exists.
- The members of the household.

**Aggregate: Household**

- It has exactly one opening party (S-004, ADR-0011).
- Every member is a party of kind natural person, inside the boundary (S-001).
- Adding a member changes no existing fact (S-001).

**Hotspots:** H-01, H-03, H-04.

## 2. Counterparties (S-002)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Register party (name, party kind†) | Party registered | the kind is `system` (ADR-0011) |

An aggregate party is registered with the same command, with kind aggregate.

**Policies**

- None. The duplicate-name warning (S-002) is a prompt before the command, not an
  invariant: two parties may share a name.

**Read models**

- Parties by name, to warn about an existing name and to choose a counterparty† later.

**Aggregate: Party**

- A party has exactly one party kind (primitives.md).
- A registered party is outside the boundary (S-002).
- Parties of kind `system` are created by the system only and are never used for
  real-world counterparties (ADR-0011).

**Hotspots:** H-04.

## 3. Accounts (S-003)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Register account (name, owners with ownership shares, optional institution†) | Account registered | shares do not sum to 100%, with a typed error naming the actual sum (S-003) |

**Read models**

- Members, to choose owners.
- Parties that can be an institution.

**Aggregate: Account**

- Ownership shares sum to 100% (conventions.md, S-003).
- The institution is optional (primitives.md, S-003).
- In R1 the account holds only EUR (S-003, r1.md).
- It has at most one opening balance (primitives.md, ADR-0011). See section 4.

**Hotspots:** H-04, H-05, H-06.

## 4. Opening balance (S-004)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Record opening balance (account, date, amount) | Opening balance recorded | the account already has one (S-004) |

**Policies**

- Whenever an opening balance is recorded, then a checkpoint taken as given and a
  transaction between the account and the opening party are recorded at that date, so
  that the derived position† equals the checkpoint (ADR-0011).

**Read models**

- Per account: whether it has an opening balance, and its date.

**Aggregate and invariants**

- At most one opening balance per account (S-004, ADR-0011).
- The counterparty of the opening transaction is the household's opening party
  (ADR-0011).
- The derived position at the opening date equals the opening checkpoint, and positions
  remain the sum of flows (S-004, ADR-0011).
- Flows against the opening party never count as in or out (S-004, ADR-0011).
- Which aggregate holds the "at most one" rule, and whether an opening balance is one
  fact or two linked facts, is H-09.

**Hotspots:** H-08, H-09, H-10.

## 5. Recording flows (S-005, S-006, S-007)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Record transaction (valid time: a date or an interval; one or more flows) | Transaction recorded | a flow is dated before the opening balance of an account it touches (S-005); the interval ends before it starts (S-006); the flows do not net to zero per unit (primitives.md) |

Income, spending and transfers are recorded with the same command. What a flow is
follows from its two ends, it is not a stored type:

- Both ends are household accounts†: a transfer (glossary).
- One end is a household account, the other a party outside the boundary that is not a
  system party: in or out (S-013).
- One end is a system party: neither, it is excluded from in and out (S-013, ADR-0011).

A transaction may mix these, e.g. a cash withdrawal (transfer) with a fee (out)
(S-007).

**Policies**

- None. R1 has no deduplication and no transfer matching (r1.md, out of scope).

**Read models**

- Accounts, each with the date of its opening balance.
- Parties, to choose the counterparty.

**Aggregate: Transaction**

- Its flows net to zero per unit (primitives.md, S-005).
- All its flows share its valid time, which is a civil date or a closed interval of
  civil dates (ADR-0009).
- An aggregate flow is stored as one flow with its interval and is never prorated
  (ADR-0009, S-006).
- In R1 every flow is in EUR (r1.md).
- It carries a valid time and a recorded time (S-005, r1.md).
- No flow is dated before the opening balance of an account it touches (S-005,
  OQ-019). This needs a fact from outside the transaction: the account's opening date.
- A transaction recorded by a member never has a system party as an end (ADR-0011).

**Derived, not events**

- Derived position of an account as of a date: the sum of its flows (ADR-0011).
- Approximate: the as-of date lies strictly inside the interval of an aggregate flow on
  the account; the aggregate is then not included (ADR-0009, S-006).

**Hotspots:** H-07, H-08, H-10, H-11, H-12, H-13.

## 6. Corrections (S-008)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Correct transaction (the whole transaction, restated) | Transaction corrected | the new version breaks a Transaction invariant, e.g. does not net to zero (S-008); the transaction is void (ADR-0010) |
| Void transaction | Transaction voided | it is already void (ADR-0010) |
| Correct opening balance | Opening balance corrected | H-09 |
| Correct checkpoint | Checkpoint corrected | H-18 |
| Void checkpoint | Checkpoint voided | H-18 |

**Policies**

- Whenever an opening balance is corrected, then its transaction and its checkpoint
  each get a new version (ADR-0011).

**Read models**

- The current version of the fact to be corrected.
- Figures as known on† a recorded time (S-008). See H-16.
- A way to find the transaction in the first place. See H-17.

**Aggregate: Transaction (versions)**

- A correction is a new version of the whole transaction. Flows are never corrected on
  their own (ADR-0010).
- Every version satisfies the Transaction invariants of section 5 (S-008).
- Earlier versions stay stored and are never modified (ADR-0010, S-008).
- Voiding is a final version; no version follows it (ADR-0010).
- Corrections apply retroactively in valid time and create no reversing flow
  (ADR-0010, S-008).
- A refund is not a correction. It is a new transaction (ADR-0010).

**Hotspots:** H-09, H-14, H-15, H-16, H-17, H-18.

## 7. Classification (S-009, S-010)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Define purpose† (name) | Purpose defined | the name already exists (S-009) |
| Rename purpose | Purpose renamed | not stated; presumably the new name already exists (H-04) |
| Assign purpose to a flow | Purpose assigned | H-14 |

Changing a flow's purpose is the same command as assigning one.

**Policies**

- Whenever a purpose is assigned to a flow that already has one, then the assignment
  gets a new version and the previous one is kept with its recorded time (S-010,
  ADR-0010).

**Read models**

- The purposes of the household.
- Flows without a purpose (Unclassified†), and a flow's current purpose.

**Aggregates**

- Purpose (the household's vocabulary): names are unique within the household; a
  purpose keeps its identity when renamed, so assignments keep pointing to it (S-009).
- Dimension assignment†: links a flow to a purpose and carries provenance, here
  "assigned by the member the request is served for, confirmed" (S-010, S-014). It is
  a versioned fact (ADR-0010). A flow has at most one current purpose (implied by
  S-010, "change the purpose").

**Hotspots:** H-02, H-04, H-14, H-17.

## 8. Checkpoints (S-011)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Record checkpoint (account, date, amount) | Checkpoint recorded | it is dated before the account's opening balance (S-011) |

**Policies**

- None, on purpose. Whenever a checkpoint shows a difference, nothing is booked (S-011,
  r1.md). Booking it is R2 (H-20).

**Read models**

- The derived position of the account at the checkpoint date, with its approximate
  marking.
- The checkpoint difference†: checkpoint minus derived position (S-011: 260.00 against
  310.00 shows -50.00). It is derived each time, never stored, so it changes when
  flows are recorded, corrected or voided (S-011). Approximate if the date lies inside
  an aggregate flow's interval on that account (ADR-0009, S-011).

**Aggregate: Checkpoint**

- It is not dated before the account's opening balance (S-011).
- It is a versioned fact (ADR-0010).
- It never replaces the derived position (primitives.md).

**Hotspots:** H-08, H-10, H-11, H-17, H-18, H-19, H-20.

## 9. Views (S-012, S-013)

**Actor:** member, reading. No commands and no events.

**Read models**

| View | Shows | Selected by |
|---|---|---|
| Balance sheet† | Derived position per household account, net worth†, unexplained checkpoint differences | as-of date; household or member |
| In and out† | Inflows and outflows by purpose, Unclassified included; per member also opening, between members and closing | period; household or member |

Both use current knowledge by default (conventions.md). "As known on" is H-16.

**Rules the views apply**

- Derived position: sum of flows up to the as-of date (ADR-0011).
- Net worth: sum of all positions of household accounts, equal to the sum of the
  per-member figures (S-012).
- Member figures attribute positions and flows through ownership shares (S-012, S-013,
  primitives.md).
- Transfers are excluded from household in and out (S-007, S-013). In member views they
  appear only on the between-members line (ADR-0012).
- Flows against a system party are excluded from every in and out view (S-013,
  ADR-0011).
- A flow without a purpose appears under Unclassified, never dropped (S-010, S-013).
- An aggregate flow counts in full if the period contains its interval; if the period
  only partly overlaps it, it is excluded, the figure is partial and the aggregate is
  named (ADR-0009, S-013).
- Balance sheet as of a date before an account's opening balance: the account is
  unknown, not zero (S-012). No opening balance at all: shown as having no starting
  point (S-004).
- In and out for a period starting before an account's opening balance: partial for
  that account (S-013).
- Per member: opening + in - out + between members = closing; between-members lines
  sum to zero across the household; members' in and out sum to the household's
  (ADR-0012, S-013).

**Where the rules live**

- In the backend, never in the frontend (conventions.md, Frontend).
- The exclusion of flows against system parties belongs to the Ledger context, not to
  individual views (ADR-0011).

**Hotspots:** H-05, H-06, H-08, H-16, H-21, H-22.

## 10. Access (S-014)

**Actors:** member (through a browser), Cloudflare Access (external system).

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Any request | Request served for a member | there is no valid Access JWT, even if an email header is present; or the email maps to no member (S-014) |

**Policies**

- Whenever a request arrives, then its Access JWT is verified and its email is mapped
  to a member before anything else happens (S-014, r1.md enablers).

**Read models**

- Email to member.

**Aggregate**

- None identified. Who owns the email-to-member mapping is H-02.

**Hotspots:** H-01, H-02.

## Proposed glossary entries

Terms the stories, ADRs or this document use that the glossary does not define.
Proposals only; they move to `docs/glossary.md` after review. Entries that depend on a
hotspot say so.

| Term | Proposed definition | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| Party kind | What sort of party it is: natural person, legal entity, institution, public body, aggregate or system. | primitives.md, S-001, S-002 |
| Counterparty | The party at the other end of a flow, seen from a household account. | primitives.md, S-002, S-005 |
| Institution | The party an account is held at, if any. | glossary (Account), S-003 |
| Household account | Depends on H-05. | glossary (Transfer), S-007, S-012 |
| Derived position | The position of an account as of a date, computed as the sum of its flows. | S-004 to S-006, S-011, ADR-0009, ADR-0011 |
| Checkpoint difference | A checkpoint minus the derived position at the same date. | S-011, S-012 |
| Purpose | A dimension, and in R1 the only one: what a flow was for, from a vocabulary the household defines. | S-009, S-010, r1.md |
| Unclassified | A flow with no current purpose assignment. | S-010, S-013 |
| Dimension assignment | The fact that links a dimension value to a flow, with provenance. | primitives.md, ADR-0010 |
| Balance sheet | The view of derived positions per account as of a date, for the household or a member. | S-012 |
| Net worth | The sum of all positions of household accounts as of a date. | S-012 |
| In and out | The view of inflows and outflows by purpose over a period. In: flows into a household account from a party outside the boundary. Out: the reverse. Flows against system parties are neither. Depends on H-22. | S-013, ADR-0011, ADR-0012 |
| Unknown (figure) | A figure that cannot be stated, e.g. a position as of a date before the account's opening balance. Never shown as zero. | S-012, ADR-0009 |
| Exact (figure) | A figure that is neither approximate, partial nor unknown. | ADR-0009 |
| As known on | A query that uses the versions that were current at a given recorded time, instead of current knowledge. | conventions.md, ADR-0010, S-008 |
| Reporting currency | The household's single currency for views. EUR in R1. | primitives.md, r1.md |
