# Event storming: R1 (manual ledger)

Status: draft, under review. Reviewed so far: what a flow is (H-07, ADR-0013), the
day boundary (H-08, ADR-0014), the life of an opening balance (H-09, H-10,
ADR-0015), purposes on flows (H-14, H-24, ADR-0016, ADR-0017), member figures
(H-06, H-25, ADR-0018, ADR-0019), the views (H-21, H-22, ADR-0020 to ADR-0022) and
the household (H-01 to H-05, H-30, ADR-0023 to ADR-0029).

Written event storming against the R1 stories (S-001 to S-015,
[story-map.md](../story-map.md#stories)). It is the input for the context map in
`architecture.md`. Nothing here decides an open point: conflicts, unknowns and "it
depends" are collected under [Hotspots](#hotspots), and each points to an ADR or an
open question.

## How to read this

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Actor | Who issues a command. |
| Command | A request to change something. It may be rejected. |
| Event | Something that happened, in past tense. |
| Policy | "Whenever X, then Y": a reaction the system performs without being asked. |
| Read model | What an actor must see before deciding, or what a view shows. |
| Aggregate | The consistency boundary that accepts or rejects a command, with the invariants it enforces. |
| Hotspot | A conflict, unknown or "it depends". `H-nn`, listed at the end. |

- Events are a modelling device here. Whether any of them is persisted as an event is
  not decided (H-23).
- Every rule cites the story, ADR or spec it comes from. A rule without a source is a
  hotspot, not a rule.
- Examples use the reference household (`fixtures/README.md`).
- Terms marked † are not in the glossary yet; see
  [Proposed glossary entries](#proposed-glossary-entries).

## Big picture

Events in the order a household meets them:

1. Household created, Opening party created, Member added, Login identity mapped
2. Party registered, Implicit account created
3. Account registered
4. Opening balance recorded
5. Transaction recorded
6. Purpose defined, Purpose renamed, Purpose assigned, Purpose removed
7. Checkpoint recorded
8. Transaction corrected, Transaction voided, Opening balance corrected, Opening
   balance voided, Checkpoint corrected, Checkpoint voided, Party corrected, Account
   corrected, Party voided, Account voided, Purpose voided

What must exist first:

- Account registered needs its owners (Member added) and, if it has one, its
  institution (Party registered).
- Opening balance recorded needs the account and the opening party.
- Transaction recorded needs the accounts its flows are on (for a counterparty, its
  implicit account), and no flow may be dated on or before the opening balance of
  the account it is on (S-005, ADR-0014). A household
  account needs an opening balance before it takes any flow (ADR-0015).
- Purpose assigned needs the purpose and a flow that can carry one (ADR-0016).
- Checkpoint recorded needs the account and its opening balance (ADR-0015), and must
  not be dated before it (S-011).

The only actor in R1 is a member, plus Cloudflare Access as an external system
(S-014). Nothing in R1 is triggered by time.

## 1. Household (S-001)

**Actor:** member. Before any member exists: whoever is signed in with the email named
for first setup (ADR-0024).

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Run first setup (first member) | Household created; Opening party created; Implicit account created (for the opening party); Member added; Login identity mapped | a household already exists; the request does not come from the email named for first setup (ADR-0024) |
| Add member | Member added | not stated |

**Policies**

- Whenever a household is created, then its opening party is created (S-004,
  ADR-0011), and with it the opening party's implicit account (ADR-0013).

**Read models**

- Whether a household exists.
- The members of the household.

**Aggregate: Household**

- It has exactly one opening party (S-004, ADR-0011).
- An instance holds one household (ADR-0024).
- Every member is a party of kind natural person, inside the boundary (S-001).
- The boundary is the set of members. It is not edited on its own, and the household
  is not a party (ADR-0023).
- Adding a member changes no existing fact (S-001).
- A member gets no implicit account and is never a counterparty directly (ADR-0013).

**Hotspots:** none open. H-01, H-03 and H-04 are resolved (ADR-0023 to ADR-0025).

## 2. Counterparties (S-002)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Register party (name, party kind†) | Party registered; Implicit account created | the kind is `system` (ADR-0011) |

An aggregate party is registered with the same command, with kind aggregate.

**Policies**

- Whenever a party outside the boundary is created, system parties included, then its
  implicit account is created, owned by that party (ADR-0013).
- The duplicate-name warning (S-002) is not a policy. It is a prompt before the
  command, and not an invariant: two parties may share a name. Names are compared
  ignoring case, after trimming (ADR-0029).

**Read models**

- Parties by name, to warn about an existing name and to choose a counterparty† later.

**Aggregate: Party**

- A party has exactly one party kind (primitives.md).
- A registered party is outside the boundary (S-002).
- Every party can own accounts. A party outside the boundary has exactly one implicit
  account (ADR-0013).
- Parties of kind `system` are created by the system only and are never used for
  real-world counterparties (ADR-0011).

**Hotspots:** none open. H-04 is resolved (ADR-0025).

## 3. Accounts (S-003)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Register account (name, owners with ownership shares, optional institution†) | Account registered | shares do not sum to 100%, with a typed error naming the actual sum (S-003); a member and a party outside the boundary would both be owners (ADR-0023); the institution is not a party of kind institution (ADR-0027) |

**Read models**

- Parties, to choose owners. Every party can own accounts (ADR-0013), but the owners
  of one account are either all members or all outside the boundary (ADR-0023).
- Parties that can be an institution.

**Aggregate: Account**

- Ownership shares are exact fractions and sum to exactly 1 (conventions.md, S-003,
  ADR-0019).
- The institution is optional (primitives.md, S-003). If given, it is a party of kind
  institution (ADR-0027).
- A household account is an account all of whose owners are members (ADR-0023).
- In R1 the account holds only EUR (S-003, r1.md).
- It has at most one non-void opening balance. The Account enforces this (ADR-0011,
  ADR-0015). See section 4.
- An implicit account is created by the system, not registered, and is owned by its
  party alone (ADR-0013).

**Hotspots:** none open. H-04, H-05 and H-06 are resolved (ADR-0019, ADR-0023, ADR-0025,
ADR-0027).

## 4. Opening balance (S-004)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Record opening balance (account, date, amount) | Opening balance recorded | the account already has a non-void one (S-004, ADR-0015) |

**Policies**

- Whenever an opening balance is recorded, then a checkpoint taken as given and a
  transaction are recorded at that date, so that the derived position† equals the
  checkpoint (ADR-0011). The transaction has two flows: the amount on the account and
  the opposite amount on the opening party's implicit account (ADR-0013).

**Read models**

- Per account: whether it has an opening balance, and its date.

**Aggregate and invariants**

- At most one non-void opening balance per account, enforced by the Account (S-004,
  ADR-0011, ADR-0015).
- An opening balance is one fact with its own identity and versions. Its checkpoint
  and its transaction are parts, written together and never changed on their own
  (ADR-0015).
- A household account takes no flow and no checkpoint without an opening balance
  (ADR-0015).
- An opening balance at a date is the account's end-of-day balance of that date. Apart
  from the opening transaction, flows on the account are dated after it (ADR-0014).
- The opposite flow of the opening transaction is on the implicit account of the
  household's opening party (ADR-0011, ADR-0013).
- The derived position at the opening date equals the opening checkpoint, and positions
  remain the sum of flows (S-004, ADR-0011).
- Flows on the opening party's account never count as in or out (S-004, ADR-0011).
- A new account is not a special case: the entry form's "new account, opened on D"
  records an opening balance of 0.00 for the end of the day before D (ADR-0015).

**Hotspots:** none open. H-08, H-09 and H-10 are resolved (ADR-0014, ADR-0015).

## 5. Recording flows (S-005, S-006, S-007)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Record transaction (valid time: a date or an interval; two or more flows, each an account and a signed amount) | Transaction recorded | the flows do not net to zero per unit (ADR-0013); a flow is on a household account without an opening balance (ADR-0015); a flow is dated on or before the opening balance of the account it is on (S-005, ADR-0014); the interval ends before it starts (S-006) |

Income, spending and transfers are recorded with the same command. A flow is signed
and on one account (ADR-0013). What it means for the household follows from the
account it is on, it is not a stored type:

- On a non-household account, in a transaction that also has a flow on a household
  account†: out if positive, in if negative (ADR-0013).
- On a system party's account: excluded from in and out (S-013, ADR-0011).
- A transaction whose flows are all on household accounts is a transfer (glossary) and
  adds nothing to in or out.
- A transaction with no flow on a household account is allowed and affects no
  household view (ADR-0013).

A transaction may mix these, e.g. a cash withdrawal with a fee: Alex's current account
-202.00, Alex's cash wallet +200.00, Example Bank +2.00 (S-007, ADR-0013). Only the
2.00 is out.

Members do not enter signed lines. R1 entry forms start from a household account and a
counterparty; the flows are composed from that (ADR-0013).

**Policies**

- None. R1 has no deduplication and no transfer matching (r1.md, out of scope).

**Read models**

- Accounts, each with the date of its opening balance.
- Parties, to choose the counterparty.

**Aggregate: Transaction**

- Its flows net to zero per unit. This is checked for every version and can fail
  (ADR-0013, S-005, S-008).
- Every flow is on an account, never directly against a party (ADR-0013).
- It need not have a flow on a household account (ADR-0013).
- All its flows share its valid time, which is a civil date or a closed interval of
  civil dates (ADR-0009).
- An aggregate flow is stored as one flow with its interval and is never prorated
  (ADR-0009, S-006).
- In R1 every flow is in EUR (r1.md).
- It carries a valid time and a recorded time (S-005, r1.md).
- No flow is dated on or before the opening balance of the account it is on, the
  opening transaction excepted (S-005, OQ-019, ADR-0014). This needs a fact from
  outside the transaction: the account's opening date.
  Implicit accounts have no opening balance and accept flows without one (ADR-0013).
- No flow is on a household account without an opening balance, also not as the
  destination of a transfer (ADR-0015).
- A transaction recorded by a member never has a flow on a system party's account
  (ADR-0011).
- It may carry an optional occurred-at, which orders transactions within one valid
  date and never changes it. No R1 feature uses it (ADR-0014).

**Derived, not events**

- Derived position of an account as of a date: the sum of its flows with a valid date
  on or before it, i.e. its end-of-day position (ADR-0011, ADR-0014).
- Movement: units going from one account to another, derived from the flows of a
  transaction, never stored (ADR-0013). No R1 view needs it.
- Approximate: the as-of date is on or after the first day and before the last day of
  the interval of an aggregate flow on the account; the aggregate is then not included
  (ADR-0009, ADR-0014, S-006).

**Hotspots:** H-11, H-12, H-13. H-07, H-08, H-10 and H-25 are resolved (ADR-0013,
ADR-0014, ADR-0015, ADR-0018).

## 6. Corrections (S-008, S-015)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Correct transaction (the whole transaction, restated) | Transaction corrected | the new version breaks a Transaction invariant, e.g. does not net to zero (S-008); the transaction is void (ADR-0010) |
| Void transaction | Transaction voided | it is already void (ADR-0010) |
| Correct opening balance | Opening balance corrected | the new date is on or after the date of a flow or checkpoint on the account, with a typed error naming them (ADR-0015) |
| Void opening balance | Opening balance voided | the account has other facts (ADR-0015) |
| Correct checkpoint | Checkpoint corrected | H-18 |
| Void checkpoint | Checkpoint voided | H-18 |
| Correct party (name, kind) | Party corrected | the kind would change to or from system; the party would cross the boundary (ADR-0025) |
| Correct account (name, institution, owners and ownership shares) | Account corrected | the shares do not sum to 1 (ADR-0019); the owners would be on both sides of the boundary (ADR-0023) |
| Void party, account or purpose | Party voided; Account voided; Purpose voided | a current fact refers to it (ADR-0025) |

**Policies**

- Whenever an opening balance is corrected, then its checkpoint and its transaction
  are written together as parts of the new version (ADR-0011, ADR-0015).
- Whenever a correction removes a flow or leaves it unable to carry a purpose, or a
  transaction is voided, then the purpose assignments on those flows stop being
  current. They stay stored. The UI warns before saving (ADR-0016, ADR-0017).

**Read models**

- The current version of the fact to be corrected.
- Figures as known on† a recorded time (S-008). See H-16.
- A way to find the transaction in the first place. See H-17.

**Aggregate: Transaction (versions)**

- A correction is a new version of the whole transaction. Flows are never corrected on
  their own (ADR-0010).
- A flow keeps a stable ID within its transaction across versions. A correction states
  for each flow whether it continues an existing one (ADR-0016).
- A correction may change anything but the fact's identity, as long as the new version
  satisfies all rules and breaks no independent fact (ADR-0017).
- Every version satisfies the Transaction invariants of section 5 (S-008).
- Earlier versions stay stored and are never modified (ADR-0010, S-008).
- Voiding is a final version; no version follows it (ADR-0010).
- Corrections apply retroactively in valid time and create no reversing flow
  (ADR-0010, S-008).
- A refund is not a correction. It is a new transaction (ADR-0010).
- A correction or void never invalidates independent recorded facts (other
  transactions, checkpoints). One that would is rejected with a typed error naming
  them. Dependent facts, such as purpose assignments on its flows, follow their parent
  and never block it (ADR-0015, ADR-0016). For an opening balance: the
  amount can always be corrected, the date only to an earlier date or to one before
  every flow and checkpoint on the account, and it can be voided only while the
  account has no other facts.
- Parties, accounts, ownership shares and purposes are versioned like facts. A
  corrected ownership share applies to all dates; "as known on" shows the earlier
  view. A party's implicit account is voided with it (ADR-0025).

**Hotspots:** H-15, H-16, H-17, H-18.

## 7. Classification (S-009, S-010)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Define purpose† (name) | Purpose defined | the name is the name of another purpose, ignoring case and surrounding whitespace (S-009, ADR-0029) |
| Rename purpose | Purpose renamed | the new name is the name of another purpose (ADR-0029) |
| Assign purpose to a flow | Purpose assigned | the flow cannot carry a purpose: it is on a household account or on a system party's account, or its transaction has no flow on a household account (ADR-0016) |
| Remove purpose from a flow | Purpose removed | not stated |

Changing a flow's purpose is the same command as assigning one. Removing it leaves the
flow Unclassified; the previous assignment is kept in history (ADR-0017).

A purpose attaches to a single flow, and only to a flow that counts in in and out: one
on the account of a party outside the boundary that is not a system party, in a
transaction that has a flow on a household account. A split lives on that side: joint
current account -80.00, Corner Grocer +50.00 (groceries) and +30.00 (household goods).
Members classify their statement line; the UI writes the assignments to the
counterparty-side flows. Aggregate flows follow the same rule (ADR-0016).

**Policies**

- Whenever a purpose is assigned to a flow that already has one, then the assignment
  gets a new version and the previous one is kept with its recorded time (S-010,
  ADR-0010).

**Read models**

- The purposes of the household.
- Flows that can carry a purpose and have none (Unclassified†), and a flow's current
  purpose.

**Aggregates**

- Purpose (the household's vocabulary): names are unique within the household,
  stored trimmed and compared ignoring case (ADR-0029); a
  purpose keeps its identity when renamed, so assignments keep pointing to it (S-009).
- Dimension assignment†: links a purpose to one flow, referenced by transaction and
  flow ID (ADR-0016). It is a dependent fact: it keeps its flow through an amount
  correction, stays with one part when the flow is split, and stops being current
  when the flow is removed or the transaction voided (ADR-0016). It carries
  provenance, here
  "assigned by the member the request is served for, confirmed, confidence certain"
  (S-010, S-014, ADR-0017). It is
  a versioned fact (ADR-0010). A flow has at most one current purpose (implied by
  S-010, "change the purpose").

**Hotspots:** H-17. H-02, H-04, H-14 and H-24 are resolved (ADR-0016, ADR-0017, ADR-0024,
ADR-0025, ADR-0029).

## 8. Checkpoints (S-011)

**Actor:** member.

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Record checkpoint (account, date, amount) | Checkpoint recorded | the account has no opening balance (ADR-0015); it is dated before the account's opening balance (S-011) |

**Policies**

- None, on purpose. Whenever a checkpoint shows a difference, nothing is booked (S-011,
  r1.md). Booking it is R2 (H-20).

**Read models**

- The derived position of the account at the checkpoint date, with its approximate
  marking.
- The checkpoint difference†: checkpoint minus derived position (S-011: 260.00 against
  310.00 shows -50.00). It is derived each time, never stored, so it changes when
  flows are recorded, corrected or voided (S-011). Approximate if the date lies inside
  an aggregate flow's interval on that account (ADR-0009, S-011).
- The checkpoints of an account, each with its date, amount and current difference.
  An earlier difference stays visible at its own date when a later checkpoint matches
  (S-011).

**Aggregate: Checkpoint**

- It is the end-of-day balance of its date (ADR-0014).
- It is not dated before the account's opening balance (S-011).
- It is a versioned fact (ADR-0010).
- It never replaces the derived position (primitives.md).

**Hotspots:** H-11, H-17, H-18, H-19, H-20.

## 9. Views (S-012, S-013)

**Actor:** member, reading. No commands and no events.

**Read models**

| View | Shows | Selected by |
|---|---|---|
| Balance sheet† | Derived position per household account, net worth†, the difference of each account's latest checkpoint on or before the as-of date | as-of date; household or member |
| In and out† | In and out netted per purpose and listed as income and spending; Unclassified as in and out separately; the reconciliation from opening to closing | period; household or member |

Both use current knowledge by default (conventions.md). "As known on" is H-16.

**Rules the views apply**

- Derived position: sum of flows with a valid date on or before the as-of date
  (ADR-0011, ADR-0014).
- A period's opening figure is the end-of-day position on the day before the period;
  its closing figure is the end-of-day position on its last day (ADR-0014).
- Net worth: sum of all positions of household accounts, equal to the sum of the
  per-member figures (S-012).
- A figure is a value plus flags: approximate, and partial with named exclusions. A
  total takes the union. An unknown part is left out and named, never counted as zero
  (ADR-0021).
- Household in and out are the flows on non-household accounts, in transactions that
  also have a flow on a household account: positive is out, negative is in (ADR-0013).
- Member figures attribute positions and flows through ownership shares (S-012, S-013,
  primitives.md). Out is attributed to the members whose money paid it, in to the
  members whose account received it: in proportion to the household flows of the
  opposite sign, or of the same sign if there are none. Between members is the
  remainder: change in attributed positions, minus in, plus out (ADR-0018).
- Each flow is allocated to members in whole minor units by largest remainder, with a
  tie-break derived from the flow's ID. Member figures are sums of these allocations,
  so the reconciliation holds exactly (ADR-0019).
- Transfers add nothing to household in and out (S-007, S-013, ADR-0013). In member
  views they appear only on the between-members line (ADR-0012).
- Flows on a system party's account are neither in nor out (S-013, ADR-0011). The
  reconciliation shows them on one line per system party; in R1 that is "Opening
  balances" (ADR-0020).
- A flow that counts in in and out and has no purpose appears under Unclassified, never
  dropped (S-010, S-013, ADR-0016).
- A view by purpose nets in and out per purpose. A purpose with net in is income, one
  with net out is spending, and a refund reduces its purpose. Unclassified is shown
  as in and out separately (ADR-0022).
- An aggregate flow counts in full if the period contains its interval; if the period
  only partly overlaps it, it is excluded, and the line of its purpose and the totals are
  flagged partial, naming the aggregate (ADR-0009, ADR-0021, S-013).
- Balance sheet as of a date before an account's opening balance: the account is
  unknown, not zero, and net worth is flagged partial, naming it (S-012, ADR-0021).
  No opening balance at all: shown as having no starting point (S-004).
- In and out for a period starting on or before an account's opening balance: the view
  is flagged partial once, naming the account as not tracked (S-013, ADR-0014,
  ADR-0021).
- Per member and for the household: tracked opening + opening balances + in - out +
  between members = closing. An account not yet tracked at the start of the period is
  left out of the opening figure and named (ADR-0020). Between-members lines
  sum to zero across the household; members' in and out sum to the household's
  (ADR-0012, S-013).

**Where the rules live**

- In the backend, never in the frontend (conventions.md, Frontend).
- The exclusion of flows on system parties' accounts belongs to the Ledger context, not to
  individual views (ADR-0011).

**Hotspots:** H-05, H-16. H-06, H-21, H-22 and H-25 are resolved (ADR-0018 to ADR-0022).

## 10. Access (S-014)

**Actors:** member (through a browser), Cloudflare Access (external system).

| Command | Events | Rejected when |
|---|---|---|
| Any request | Request served for a member | there is no valid Access JWT, even if an email header is present; or the email is no login identity of a member (S-014) |
| Map login identity to a member | Login identity mapped | it is already mapped to another member (ADR-0024) |
| Remove login identity | Login identity removed | it is the household's last login identity (ADR-0026) |

**Policies**

- Whenever a request arrives, then its Access JWT is verified and its email is mapped
  to a member before anything else happens (S-014, r1.md enablers).
- Whenever a member's command records a version of a fact, then the version records
  that member next to its recorded time (ADR-0028). Sign-ins and denied requests are
  not events of the domain: operational logs only.

**Read models**

- Login identity to member.

**Aggregate**

- Login identity: maps to exactly one member; a member may have several, or none. It
  is kept apart from the member (ADR-0024). A household always has at least one
  (ADR-0026).

**Hotspots:** none open. H-30 is resolved (ADR-0026).
H-01 and H-02 are resolved (ADR-0024, ADR-0028).

## Proposed glossary entries

Terms the stories, ADRs or this document use that the glossary does not define.
Proposals only; they move to `docs/glossary.md` after review. Entries that depend on a
hotspot say so. Movement and implicit account are already in the glossary with
ADR-0013, occurred-at with ADR-0014.

| Term | Proposed definition | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| Party kind | What sort of party it is: natural person, legal entity, institution, public body, aggregate or system. | primitives.md, S-001, S-002 |
| Counterparty | Seen from a household account: a party outside the boundary that owns an account with a flow in the same transaction. | primitives.md, S-002, S-005 |
| Institution | The party an account is held at, if any. Always of kind institution (ADR-0027). | glossary (Account), S-003 |
| Derived position | The position of an account as of a date, computed as the sum of its flows with a valid date on or before it. | S-004 to S-006, S-011, ADR-0009, ADR-0011 |
| Checkpoint difference | A checkpoint minus the derived position at the same date. | S-011, S-012 |
| Purpose | A dimension, and in R1 the only one: what a flow was for, from a vocabulary the household defines. Assigned to a single flow that counts in in and out (ADR-0016). | S-009, S-010, r1.md |
| Unclassified | A flow that can carry a purpose and has no current purpose assignment. | S-010, S-013 |
| Dimension assignment | The fact that links a dimension value to a flow, with provenance. A dependent fact: it follows its flow when the transaction is corrected or voided. | primitives.md, ADR-0010, ADR-0016 |
| Balance sheet | The view of derived positions per account as of a date, for the household or a member. | S-012 |
| Net worth | The sum of all positions of household accounts as of a date. | S-012 |
| In and out (view) | The view of a period: in and out netted per purpose and listed as income and spending, with the reconciliation from opening to closing. The terms in, out, income and spending are in the glossary (ADR-0022). | S-013 |
| Exact (figure) | A figure without flags. | ADR-0009, ADR-0021 |
| As known on | A query that uses the versions that were current at a given recorded time, instead of current knowledge. | conventions.md, ADR-0010, S-008 |
| Reporting currency | The household's single currency for views. EUR in R1. | primitives.md, r1.md |
